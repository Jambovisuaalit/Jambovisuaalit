GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked.

Will we have the whole apartment?

Yes. The entire apartment is exclusively yours for the duration of your stay.

Is there a separate bedroom?

No. There is a sleeping alcove without a separate bedroom door. The living area has a convertible 140 cm sofa bed.

How many guests can stay?

Up to four. The space is most comfortable for two; opening the sofa bed reduces the available living area.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is from 17:00 and check-out is by 11:00. Detailed arrival instructions are provided with your booking.

Where can I see the total price and cancellation terms?

Choose your dates and guest count on Airbnb or Booking.com to see the final price and conditions before confirming.

Can I book directly through this website?

For now, bookings are completed through Airbnb or Booking.com. This website introduces the apartment and links to the booking platforms.
